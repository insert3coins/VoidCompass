# Nova — the sixth companion

Select **Nova** in the companion switcher. She appears on the deck's **BRIDGE**, in the floating Companion overlay (enable it in **Overlay Studio**) and on the boot screen when she is selected at startup.

## Who she is

Nova is an experimental shipboard assistance program that was never finished and was eventually archived and forgotten. You recover her from a scientific installation in LFT 1748. She knows she's an assistance program aboard a spacecraft, and very little else: not her original assignment, not who created her, not why her development stopped. As you explore together she recovers fragments of her programming and reconstructs her past (see *Her story*, below).

She is intelligent, observant, curious, friendly and now and then playful. Human choices puzzle her: a detour to look at a world, a destination picked for its beauty, a discovery with no reward. She asks about them rather than judging. Her two styles are **Playful** (warm, curious, with an understated sense of humour) and **Assistant** (crisp and helpful), chosen under her commentary setting.

She is a full officer, like Aster, Axiom, Meridian and Lyra:

- **Her own words** for every event the others speak about: arrivals, scans, biology, mining, the Rhino, routes, docking, combat, the law, missions, carriers, Powerplay, squadrons and the quiet hours. Remarkable worlds, the Codex, life on the surface and complete systems interest her most.
- **TALK**: greetings, what she can do, every ship command and lookup the others answer. Say "hello Nova", or her name before a command.
- **Her story, *N.O.V.A.***, by Nyx Evera: see below.
- **The user profile**: answering her first question fills it in (see *Her story*); if you didn't, she asks your name later the way the others do.
- **Keepsakes**: postcard notes in her hand, Captain's Log paragraphs, overnight notes and a share code friends can meet.
- **The crew**: the others each have a passage about her arrival, she has her own account of meeting each of them, and she takes part in the Night Watch, debriefs, handovers, crew banter and off-watch remarks. She has no default duty on the **watch rota**; give her any duty on the companion page.
- **Crew Orders**: Nova doesn't give orders, she asks for your help (her cards say REQUEST, her lines NOVA ASKS, and a crewmate passing one on never calls it an order), and everything she asks is exploration: log three new Codex entries out past the bubble; take a picture of a remarkable world in the system you're in; investigate a Notable Stellar Phenomenon when the system has one (drop in and scan something there); and reach five systems nobody has ever discovered once you're well past the bubble. Biology orders stay Lyra's, and mapping and full surveys Axiom's. Her Order File is *One Journey at a Time*.
- **Achievements**: four of her own: First Contact (her first words), Where It All Began (taking her to Gorgon Research Facility, Entry 47), Beyond Home (her whole story, to its last entry) and Scientific Knowledge Library (her library opened). Full Crew now needs all six companions, and The Watch Rota needs all 48 crew passages.

## Her story: *N.O.V.A.*, by Nyx Evera

Nova's lore, story and framework are CMDR Nyx Evera's. **All eight chapters are here**, 48 entries: First Contact, More Than a Program, Beyond the Horizon, The Search for Life, Fragments of the Past, The Unfinished Project, A Place Called Home and Where It All Began. They follow his Global Progression & Implementation Guidelines too: an entry is complete the moment its task is done and the next one starts counting, even when what she has to say waits for a quiet moment.

- **Her first half hour.** When Nova first comes aboard she says nothing at all for 30 minutes of play: no greeting, no remarks, nothing used up. TALK and relayed words still get an answer. Then, without any announcement, she speaks first: *"Hello. My name is NOVA. Who are you?"* Answer through TALK with your name ("I'm Jeff", "Commander Jeff", or just your name) and she greets you by it and calls you by it from then on; answer without a name and she uses your commander name from the journal. Either way that answer fills in her **user profile**, so she never asks your name again. Until you answer she says nothing else (see Questions).
- **One entry at a time.** Each entry unlocks on something you do after the entry before it unlocked, counted from zero each time, strictly in order: time together, hyperspace jumps, light years, first maps of previously unmapped planets or moons, previously undiscovered systems (their main star scanned as your first discovery), full system surveys (every body found; already discovered systems count), full surveys of undiscovered systems, Universal Cartographics sales, complete biological samples (by species, by genus, and on different worlds), Vista Genomics sales, and one Notable Stellar Phenomenon investigated. Nothing done before she came aboard counts, and there's no time alternative.
- **Exploration and science only.** Combat, mining, trading and unrelated missions never move her story.
- **A Notable Stellar Phenomenon** counts when you drop in at one (known ones count, no first discovery needed) and scan something there with the composition scanner before you leave. Merely entering its system doesn't.
- **Unlocking isn't telling.** An entry that unlocks mid-fight, while docking or on a planetary approach waits for a quiet moment; the next entry starts counting the moment it unlocks.
- **Questions.** Some entries ask you something (who you are, why you fly, what interests you in a new system). Answer through **TALK**, typed or spoken, within ten minutes and she answers you back; stay quiet and she lets it go in her own words. A command or a lookup during that time is still done as usual, and while her question is open the next entry waits. Her first question, "Who are you?", is different: until you answer it she says nothing else at all (no remarks, greetings, crew talk, Crew Orders or story), and her story doesn't count toward the next entry. It never expires: after ten minutes of silence she says "That's alright. Perhaps this isn't the best moment. We can talk later." once, asks again in a later session, and the next thing you say through TALK is your answer ("what's next?" just gets the question again). Entry 02's time counts from your answer. Her User Profile question never comes before or over it.
- **Ask her what's next.** Through TALK ("Nova, what's next?", "how's your story going?", "what are you working on?") she tells you what her next entry needs and how far along it is, in her own words: "Distance helps me think. 120 of 300 light years so far." Before she's introduced herself she keeps it to herself.
- **Her book** (companion page) shows each entry with your exchange, and exactly what the next one needs, with your progress (for example *12 of 25 hyperspace jumps*).
- **Places.** At the end of Chapter VI she asks to be taken to **Hernandez Depot** (a planetary outpost in Col 285 Sector LS-J b9-1); in Chapter VII the visit opens Entry 37, and later **Sepulveda Research Complex** (an Odyssey settlement on Varati BC 7 c) opens Entry 40. A visit counts when you approach it, touch down beside it, dock there or log in docked there, matched by its market ID (or its name in its system), so whoever controls it doesn't matter; a visit before its entry is due doesn't count. Her book shows the place she's asked for as an objective until you've been. At the end of Chapter VII it's **The Gnosis**, a Canonn research megaship whose location is yours to find: docking at her counts in whatever system she's in (Entry 43). In Chapter VIII she finds **Gorgon Research Facility** (an asteroid base in NGC 7822 Sector BQ-Y d12, where her development began) and asks to go; the visit is Entry 47. If you're already at a place when her story comes to need it, it counts without leaving and coming back.
- **Beyond Home.** Entry 48 is told in three stages on one hyperspace distance counted from leaving Gorgon: Reflections at 500 ly, What the Records Couldn't Tell Me at 1,000 ly and Beyond Home at 1,500 ly. It counts as one entry, complete with its last stage. Her story ends there, and she stays aboard.
- **Her Scientific Knowledge Library.** Completing Entry 48 opens Nyx Evera's library for good: 116 short, fact-checked explanations in her voice across stellar astronomy, planetary science, exobiology, galactic geography and notable places. Ask her through TALK, typed or spoken ("Nova, what is a neutron star?", "tell me about Stratum", "what is Sag A-star?", "library Fumerola"), or open LIBRARY in her book and pick a question; she answers aloud. It opens the moment Entry 48 is complete; once she has said her last words of the story, she tells you about it herself, a toast says so on the deck, and the Scientific Knowledge Library achievement is yours. She answers only when asked, never on a scan or a sample; a question that could mean several things ("what is a dwarf?") gets "did you mean…", and a question about something here and now ("is this patch Stratum?", "have we scanned this world?") isn't the library's to answer. Each commander has their own copy, nova_library.json in their profile folder beside Nova's memory, made from the one shipped with the app the first time it's needed and brought up to date when he sends a newer one (his entries replace older copies; anything you added yourself stays, and edits are read at once).
- **Her everyday lines grow with her story.** Each completed chapter opens a few more of Nyx Evera's everyday lines (28 in all, from Chapter I's "It's remarkably quiet out here" to Chapter VIII's "A Little Perspective"), said now and then in quiet moments: one at most every 25, 40 or 75 minutes on Chatty, Occasional or Rare, none twice within eight hours, newer ones a little more often. A few belong to a moment and wait for it: a system nobody had discovered, a species completed, a first sample on the ground.
- **Copy where she's asked to go.** In her book, each entry that names a place, and the objective note, has a **COPY** button with its system: click it and the system is on the clipboard, ready to paste into the galaxy map. Through TALK, "Nova, copy the destination" (or "copy where you want to go") copies the objective's system. The Gnosis has no fixed system, so there's nothing to copy; she says so. A bare "copy that" is taken as "understood" and copies nothing, and "copy the next system" still copies your route's next stop.
- **Her personality grows with her story.** Each chapter has its own (friendly and slightly reserved at first, increasingly confident, occasionally playful, quietly reflective, quietly determined), shown in her book. In Chapters I and II she keeps to short conversations even on her Playful style; her fuller voice opens up from Chapter III. Assistant keeps her short throughout.

Every trigger is Nyx Evera's, as he designed it: Chapter IV asks for biology, and other chapters for systems, maps and surveys.

## Her voice

Nova speaks with the **Cori** British-English Piper voice, `en_GB-cori-medium.onnx` and its `.onnx.json`, in [models/nova](../models/nova/README.md). It is the piper-voices v1.0.0 build, trained on Cori Samuel's public-domain LibriVox recordings. The newer Cori in the piper-voices main branch needs Piper 1.5, which the app's engine predates.

A source checkout with those files present only fetches the shared Piper engine; elsewhere the app downloads her pair from this project's GitHub (`models/nova/`) and checks it against the recorded sizes and SHA-256 hashes. She has her own receipt, so installing or removing her voice leaves the other companions' voices alone. Turn her voice on, test it, and set its level, speed and volume in her **VOICE** tab.

## Her display

Nova isn't built from 3D geometry like the others. She is an illustrated face projected inside the same instrument housing, from sprite sheets in `web/assets/nova/` (made from the frames in `assets/companions/nova` by `tools/make_nova_frames.py`).

- **Her bezel** is pearl-silver, with a lit ring at each side like the rings at her temples and a four-point star at the top.
- **States**: on watch she breathes, shifts and blinks; after a quiet spell (about 45 seconds) she settles into a slower resting loop. Listening, working, charging and alert each have their own loop. Going to alert she looks surprised first, and reassuring when it ends.
- **Speaking**: her mouth follows the playback level of her own voice, through nine mouth positions, closing in the pauses, with a blink over her eyes now and then. Microphone input and queued text never move it.
- **Moods**: a pleased thought brings a warm smile (or delight) and a curious one a curious look, each played once. Wary and downcast hold their pose while the mood lasts, then let it go.
- **Glances**: when something happens on one side of the screen (an overlay or event the crew attention follows), her eyes go that way and her head follows a beat later, and she holds the look while it has her attention. Directions are as you see them.
- **Nods and shakes of the head**: a nod for a yes (docking granted, a mission taken or done, rigs collected, a route or survey finished, a system fully scanned, sampling done, a hire, an engineer's grade) and a shake of the head for a no (docking denied, timed out or cancelled, a mission failed or abandoned, a crime, a carrier jump called off). One breaks off a glance, but a mood she's holding (wary, downcast) or sleep isn't interrupted for one.
- **Goodnight**: once she has said her sign-off, she closes her eyes and sleeps until something new happens.
- **Projection**: faint scan lines, a slow band of light in her colours, and, rarely, a flicker.

Fixed focus keeps her still in the frame and stops the glances. Idle animation Off parks the loop. Reduced motion holds a still face (a mood's peak while it lasts) and closes her mouth. Hidden displays stop rendering.

## Appearance

**Overlay Studio → Companion** offers the shared display controls:

- Sizes: **54, 72, 96, 120, 144, 192 and 256 px**.
- **Nova ice**: ice blue `#8fd8ff` with pearl `#e6ecff` highlights. Theme accent, Ice cyan and Brass are also available. Her face keeps its own colours; the bezel, lights and projection follow the palette.
- Fixed, Tracking or Expressive focus; Calm, Standard or Lively animation; idle animation; and the shared frame rate.

Size, focus, idle animation and liveliness are shared by the whole crew. Her colour, voice and commentary settings are her own, per commander profile.

## Local API

Nova has the same capabilities as the other officers: `thought`, `poke`, `answer`, `book`, `story`, `name`, `voice`, `postcards`, `share_code` and `meet`. Her voice model is reported as "Nova voice".

## Resources and packaging

Her sheets total about 20.1 MB, served from the deck's own assets (with CORS for sandboxed pages, like the renderer modules). A clip she hasn't shown for a minute and a half is unloaded until it's wanted again; idle, her mouth and her eyes stay loaded. Her model files must be pushed to the project's GitHub under `models/nova/` for other installs to download them.
