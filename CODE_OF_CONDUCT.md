# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Void Compass to be a project anyone can contribute to without friction: a space focused on good code and good collaboration.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and professional in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Give and accept feedback constructively.
- Focus on what's best for the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information without consent.
- Submitting malicious code — malware, backdoors, exploits, or intentionally vulnerable dependencies.
- Knowingly introducing viruses or other harmful payloads into the codebase.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, release feedback, and other public communication where someone is representing Void Compass.

## Reporting and enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not follow them, and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

For a confidential report, use GitHub's private [security advisory form](https://github.com/insert3coins/VoidCompass/security/advisories/new) and begin the report title with `Conduct:`. Do not post sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and with respect for the reporter's privacy and safety.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will consider the context and impact of a violation when deciding on a response. Responses may range from a private correction or warning through removal of content, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from project interaction.
