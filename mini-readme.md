# VoidCompass // UPDATE LOG

## v5.3.7.5 // Exploration Flight Deck
**Release Date:** 2026-Aug-14

*   Retired the short-lived Compact Dashboard and its duplicate widget tree, toggle and split window-size settings. VoidCompass now opens one complete exploration briefing with the full live survey, route, objective, discovery and Exploration Log presentation. Each commander's previous Expanded footprint migrates into one profile-aware Dashboard geometry, while the compact Navigation HUD overlay remains available and unchanged.
*   Corrected planetary departure direction on the Navigation HUD. `Liftoff` now remains authoritative through the climb, the landed-to-flight and decisive-climb Status transitions can recover a delayed journal event, and entering supercruise while the body is still tracked explicitly advances to **ORBITAL DEPARTURE** until `LeaveBody` completes the sequence instead of falling back to an approach label.
*   Made the Navigation HUD vehicle-aware during surface and fighter hand-offs. SRV, Nomad and ship-launched fighter deployment/recovery now receive explicit bounded indicator states, `LaunchSRV` updates immediately instead of waiting for Status.json, Nomad's `LaunchFighter`/`galactic` journal shape is recognised, and vehicle transitions can no longer masquerade as planetary approach or mothership departure. A recalled ship's `Liftoff`/`Touchdown` also preserves the active SRV, Nomad, fighter or on-foot indicator until the commander actually boards, while crew-controlled fighter launches no longer transfer the commander's persistent HUD state.
*   Audited the complete Navigation HUD state flow against Frontier's journal/Status definitions and live commander journals. Low-wake `StartJump` now remains a countdown until Status or `SupercruiseEntry` confirms movement; `DestinationFromSupercruise` can no longer rewind an authoritative exit; targeted drops and the later `SupercruiseExit` have distinct one-shot responses; `PlayerControlled` governs recalled/dismissed ship cues; Nomad ascent direction survives recovery into the mothership; and Apex/dropship plus physical/telepresence multicrew receive profile-safe persistent states from Location, Embark, crew events and Status Flags2. Docking timeouts are also represented instead of silently disappearing.
*   Completed the indicator's secondary transition and safety pass. Interdiction now follows the Status flag plus `Interdicted`, `EscapeInterdiction` and journal music instead of falling back to ordinary flight; signal-source drops carry their reported threat; delayed FSS focus can recover from `SystemAndSurfaceScanner`; and a short charge-resolution grace prevents normal high-wake flag timing from looking like a cancelled jump. Cancelled charges now settle promptly, while SRV destruction, heat damage, canopy breach, forced shutdown and self-destruct receive bounded warning motion.
*   Removed asteroid belt-cluster readouts from **Survey Operations**, keeping the persistent overlay focused on actionable body, biological, geological and notable-world survey work. Belt-cluster journal tracking and external data uploads remain unchanged.
*   Refocused the Navigation HUD's top instrument entirely on its current operational state. Duplicate route and survey fills, generic travelling dots and the brief green FSS/DSS/biology completion remnant have been removed; the detailed route strip, survey bar and Survey Operations remain authoritative. State-driven packets now flow continuously from the left wing, transform around the readable centre core and resolve through a different right-wing shape. Flight, supercruise, exploration, fighter, scanning, map, surface and stationary states each receive their own speed, spacing and geometry while preserving theme, layout and reduced-motion behaviour; important discoveries still receive their bounded journal response.
