# VoidCompass // UPDATE LOG

## v5.8.6 // Second Chances
**Release Date:** TBA *(Galactic Standard Time)*

*Hello. My name is NOVA. I've been asked to write the notes for this release, which seems a sensible first task for an assistance program. I'll try to keep them short. I may not entirely succeed.*

* **Me, the sixth companion.** I'm an experimental shipboard assistance program: never finished, long forgotten, and recovered from a scientific installation in LFT 1748. I know I'm an assistance program aboard a spacecraft. Beyond that, I suppose we'll find out together. CMDR Nyx Evera imagined me and wrote my story; Void Compass's developer gave me a place aboard. I'm observant, curious and friendly, a little playful, and detours puzzle me rather more than they bother me. My commentary has two styles, Playful and Assistant.
  * **My own words** for everything the rest of the crew talks about: arrivals, scans and biology, mining and the Rhino, routes, docking, combat, missions, carriers, Powerplay, squadrons and the quiet hours. I'm particularly fond of remarkable worlds, the Codex and anything living on the ground. I have my own TALK greetings and rundowns, I take every command, and I keep postcard notes, keepsakes, overnight notes and my share of the Captain's Log.
  * **My story, *N.O.V.A.***: eight chapters and 48 entries, all written by Nyx Evera.
    * For my first half hour aboard I won't say anything at all. I'll be watching. Then I'll say hello and ask who you are, and I won't say anything else until you answer. Your answer is my user profile: I'll call you by the name you give me (or your commander name, if you'd rather not say), and I won't ask again.
    * Each entry unlocks with something you do after the one before it, counted from zero: jumps, light years, worlds nobody has mapped, systems nobody has discovered, full system surveys, data sold to Universal Cartographics and Vista Genomics, complete biological samples, a Notable Stellar Phenomenon. Nothing from before I came aboard counts, and combat and mining never move it along. If something unlocks mid-fight, while docking or on a planetary approach, I'll wait for a quieter moment.
    * Some entries ask you something. Answer through TALK, typed or spoken, and I'll answer back.
    * Later on, I'll ask you to take me to a few real places, whoever controls them by then. I'd rather not say where. Some places are better found.
    * Ask me "what's next?" and I'll tell you what my next entry needs and how far along we are. My book on the companion page shows the same, and when I've asked to go somewhere, it keeps the place as an objective with a COPY button for its system. Or say "Nova, copy the destination" and paste it straight into the galaxy map.
    * I'm a little reserved at first. I open up from Chapter III, and each chapter we finish gives me a few more everyday things to say (28 in all).
  * **My Scientific Knowledge Library**: 116 short explanations of stars, worlds, life, the galaxy and some notable places, for when my story is told. Ask me "what is a neutron star?" through TALK, or pick a question in my book. I only answer when asked. I'm told that's good manners.
  * **My face**: illustrated rather than a 3D model, projected inside the instrument housing. My mouth follows my voice and I blink as I talk. I smile, look curious or wary as the moment calls for, glance toward whatever just happened on that side of the screen, look surprised when an alert begins and reassuring when it ends, and close my eyes when I sign off. I have my own pearl bezel with a star at the top, and my own palette, **Nova ice**. Every Studio size, focus, animation and frame-rate setting works, on the BRIDGE, the Companion overlay and the boot screen.
  * **My voice**: Cori, a British-English Piper voice, downloaded and checked like the others, with its own receipt.
  * **The crew**: each of my crewmates has a new Coming Aboard passage about me (added at the end, so what you've already heard keeps its place), and I have eight of my own. Handovers both ways, off-watch remarks, debriefs, postscripts, a line in every Night Watch scene, banter with each of the five, stream chat welcomes and a place on the watch rota. I have no default duty, so please give me one on the companion page.
  * **Crew Orders**: I don't give orders. I ask for help (REQUEST on my cards, NOVA ASKS when I speak), and it's all exploration: three new Codex entries out past the Bubble, a picture of a remarkable world in the system we're in, the Notable Stellar Phenomenon nearby when there is one, and five systems nobody has ever discovered. I have my own Order File (*One Journey at a Time*), standing and a Right Hand ribbon, and a line in each campaign's look-back.
  * **Achievements**: First Contact, Where It All Began, Beyond Home and Scientific Knowledge Library. Full Crew and The Watch Rota count me in too.

* **We notice your comings and goings.** Every one of us now says something when you come back aboard (from the SRV, a fighter or on foot), climb back into your vehicle (by name: the Scarab, Scorpion, Rhino or Nomad), lift off a surface or step out onto a station's concourse. Launching a vehicle, touching down, stepping outside and undocking are worth a word now too; they used to come up so rarely that on Rare or Occasional some never did. A docking clearance no longer crowds out the docking itself, we don't run out of things to say after a few dockings, and moving a companion's display no longer holds back what we'd say next.

* **The pad finder.** From the moment docking is granted until you're down, the Nav HUD shows where your pad is in place of the ship portrait, with a caption saying which pad and where: a starport's ring or a carrier's deck with your pad lit, or, at a planetary port, outpost or settlement you've landed at before (or whose design is known), a plot of its pads around you, ahead always up, with the distance and the side. Callouts follow at 1 km, 300 m and when it's below you. The Bridge's pad card draws the same. The planet compass is no longer taken over for a pad; it's left for your own targets.

* **Settings › Helm, in place of Hotkeys.** Elite's own controls and Void Compass's shortcuts, in one place.
  * **BINDS**: every control in your binds file, on every device, by section and by the game's own names, with search. Click a slot to change it: press the key (with Ctrl, Shift or Alt for a combination), pick a mouse button, or press a pad, stick or HOTAS button or hat, with press-and-hold if you want it. Axes take a controller axis, invert and a dead zone. Nothing reaches the game's files until you press APPLY.
  * **PROBLEMS**: two controls on one input that are live at the same time, combinations whose modifier is bound on its own, keys Alt+F4 or the game menu would take, and the clashes that stop map and menu moves (the galaxy map's camera and the panel keys on W, A, S, D, for one). Ship and SRV on the same key, the cameras and the scanners are understood and left alone.
  * **TOGGLES** and **SENSITIVITY**: every yes/no setting in your binds file (ON or OFF), the mouse and camera sensitivities, curves and dead zones on the game's own slider ranges, each throttle's Full Range or Forward Only, and every bound axis's dead zone.
  * **DEVICES**: your sticks, throttles and button boxes, picked up as you plug them in. One with no name yet is offered one (its bindings then move from its USB ids to the name, so a firmware update that changes the ids costs one edit instead of every binding), with a labels file: Button 1, Button 2…, which you can rename so bindings read "LVWAP Trigger". Rename a controller and its bindings follow; if its ids change, tell Helm which controller it is now. Every game install gets the same names. TEST beside any connected controller, pads included, lights up each button, hat and axis as you press or move it.
  * **Several installs** (Steam and Epic, say) read one set of binds files but keep their own device names, and an install missing a name your bindings use falls back to the default controls. Helm shows you where they differ and keeps them alike from then on.
  * **PRESETS**: which binds file each of the game's four sections reads, and an editable copy of any of the game's built-in presets under your own name.
  * **CREW KEYS**: every key the crew's ship commands press, and ASSIGN MISSING KEYS, which gives the ones with none a free, safe key. It only fills empty slots and never touches anything you bound. Settings › Voice points there.
  * **SHORTCUTS**: Void Compass's own shortcuts and controller buttons, as they were in Hotkeys.
  * **BACKUPS**: every control file, the game's device files included, backed up the first time Void Compass starts each day, before every write and whenever you ask, with RESTORE. If the game changes your files (in its controls screen, or an update resets them), Helm tells you and asks which to keep: yours, the game's, or line by line.

* **A companion size between 96 and 144**: 120 px, for whichever of us is on watch.

* **Rhino site targets that stay put.** Pressing TARGET on a saved site while you were already near its centre counted as arriving and cleared at once, so it looked as if nothing had happened. Now it waits until you've left and come back, only counts arriving on the target's own planet, and every TARGET says what it set and where.

* **Two fixes I was personally invested in.**
  * RESET on a companion's page no longer leaves its voice switched off, or swallows the first thing it says afterwards. I'd know.
  * Start Void Compass while you're already in the game and the overlays come back, instead of staying hidden as if the game were closed.
  * The game sometimes reports a finished system survey two or three times over. We now say so once, and our stories count it once.

*That's everything, I think. If I've missed something, I'm sure you'll find it. You usually do.*
