# VoidCompass // UPDATE LOG

## v5.3.7.4 // Navigation Awareness
**Release Date:** 2026-Aug-13

*   Deepened the Navigation HUD without adding another information panel. The next plotted star now carries its journal-supplied class and scoopability into both layouts, consecutive unscoopable legs are identified, learned fuel endurance can warn when the immediate leg leaves too little reserve, and short-route next pips adopt the same risk-aware colour. While scooping, restrained green flow now feeds the live fuel instrument and the shared navigation spine; ordinary flight, supercruise, exploration, scanning and surface approach each retain distinct ambient cadence.
*   Added live Status-driven surface and ship awareness. Planetary approach shows altitude plus descent, climb or hold state and animates converging approach vectors; gravity continues to load the chassis; and landing gear, cargo scoop and Analysis Mode gain compact symbol-only cues rather than extra labels. Selected local targets now sweep outward into a bounded acquisition lock, and every addition honours the active commander profile, theme, Standard/Expanded layout and reduced-motion setting.
*   Expanded journal reactions with route-diversion detection, distinct Fleet Carrier, neutron-star, white-dwarf and previously surveyed valuable-system arrival signatures, and unique first-discovery, first-footfall-candidate and valuable-world responses. The existing persistent route remains calm between events and performs its one-leg arrival handoff for every verified arrival family; startup journal recovery still does not replay old effects. Arrival star type safely falls back to the matching live NavRoute entry because Frontier's FSDJump event may omit `StarClass`, while a live FSD target supplies next-star intelligence even before or without a complete NavRoute snapshot.
*   Added a sustained **SCO OVERCHARGE** top-indicator state driven directly by Frontier's `Status.json` `Flags2` bit 20 rather than inferred heat or fuel use. Its compressed triple-chevron glyph, high-speed staggered chassis flow, loaded centre aperture and restrained turbulence shear appear only while Supercruise Overcharge is active, then morph cleanly back to normal supercruise. Removed the decorative diamond prefix from every bottom-left Navigation HUD context message in both layouts.
*   **Survey Operations** The overlay is substantially narrower and shorter; system mode keeps every known non-stellar body, uses subdued `NO SIGNALS` or `DSS REQUIRED` rows, and replaces boxy signal cells with a connected biological constellation whose green, orange, cyan-ringed and dim nodes show analysed, active, identified and unresolved signals. Organisms flow inline and completed biology dims in place. Focus mode carries the same flightpath treatment into its three-stage sample sequence and distance ruler while preserving live progress, geo evidence, values, belt contacts and notable worlds. Profile/theme awareness and the idle redraw guard remain intact.
*   Made planetary arrival a sustained, journal-truthful Navigation HUD sequence rather than a barely visible late-altitude cue. `ApproachBody` now survives normal live journal batching and immediately changes the top instrument to **ORBITAL APPROACH**; Frontier's live Status glide flag advances it to **GLIDE**, then post-glide altitude/descent drives **SURFACE APPROACH** until landing or `LeaveBody`. Converging orbital packets, fast glide streaks/gates and an altitude ladder with a responsive horizon make each phase visually distinct in both HUD layouts, while maps and FSD transitions retain priority.
*   Added an onboard Fleet Carrier transition to the same instrument. A known `CarrierJumpRequest` departure time starts a heavy **CARRIER TRANSIT** rail/aperture animation only when the active commander is actually aboard that carrier; the authoritative `CarrierJump` event then becomes a bounded green **CARRIER ARRIVAL** shock front before returning to the docked state. Passenger jumps without an owned-carrier schedule still receive the arrival cue, remote carrier movements do not impersonate cockpit travel, and startup recovery never replays either animation.
*   Completed the planetary flight cycle with a journal-backed departure sequence. `Liftoff` now advances the instrument into **SURFACE DEPARTURE**, live altitude gain drives rising ladder motion, entering supercruise becomes **ORBITAL DEPARTURE**, and `LeaveBody` closes the sequence with a bounded green planet-clear release before ordinary supercruise resumes. A decisive climb can also identify an aborted approach without waiting for touchdown, while small altitude corrections do not flip the HUD direction.

## v5.3.7.3 // Persistent Route Progress
**Release Date:** 2026-Aug-12

*   Rebuilt the Navigation HUD route strip around the complete plotted route rather than an upcoming-only slice. Completed game-route systems and custom waypoints now remain subdued in their original distance-proportional positions, the cyan current marker advances through the fixed geometry, pending pips no longer rescale toward the start after every arrival, and completed custom routes remain visible as history. Short routes retain their classic individual pips; long routes become a readable segmented overview with completed-distance cells, a compact cyan current locator ring, a destination diamond and an explicit current/total stop count instead of compressing hundreds of marks into a noisy rail. The current locator no longer needs pointers above or below the rail: the orange pending section and bounded arrival handoff communicate the next leg without crowding the START caption.
*   Refined the arrival sequence around that persistent track: CURRENT confirms at its newly advanced position, a single cyan-to-orange handoff energises only the first pending leg, and the next waypoint pulses once while all later stops remain static. The original endpoint changes to START after departure so it no longer falsely identifies every new system as the route origin.

## v5.3.7.2 // Journal-Aware Navigation
**Release Date:** 2026-Aug-11

*   Upgraded the header to **Navigation HUD v2** as one full-width tapered navigation instrument: route progress, a readable state glyph, survey knowledge, ambient activity and journal reactions now share the same data spine without a redundant title. State changes contract and reform through the centre while landed, on-foot and surface-vehicle states place a real gravity-dependent load on the lower chassis. Live sequences cover routes, jumps and arrivals, docking and surface transitions, vehicles, honks/FSS/DSS, biological and Codex discoveries, data sales, fuel, mining and safety events; rapid scan bursts coalesce, higher-priority events win within a batch, startup catch-up stays silent and every sequence remains theme-aware and reduced-motion safe.
*   Added a live **navigation-readiness layer** to the same instrument: verified Status flags now expose mass lock, FSD charge type, the actual witch-space transition and cooldown; selected local bodies, stations and POIs latch onto the destination end without duplicating the plotted system route; and a journal-confirmed neutron boost retains a paired energy spine and multiplier until the next completed hyperspace jump consumes it. The jump sequence now follows Frontier's real order—countdown, hyper charge, hyperspace, arrival, cooldown and destination supercruise—with scoped music-mood recovery when a short-lived Status update is missed. Target and boost state remain profile-safe, while boost readiness also reconstructs from startup journal replay.
*   Promoted the smaller Navigation HUD to the primary **Standard** layout for new installs and commander profiles while retaining the full display as **Expanded**. Existing profile choices remain unchanged, Overlay Layout Studio now presents the two modes directly, and a profile-aware configurable hotkey can switch layouts live without moving or duplicating the overlay.
*   Added a narrow dark separation layer beneath Navigation HUD tracer tails and moving data lights, keeping route, survey and journal animations visible when their theme colour matches the illuminated rail beneath them without making the centre instrument brighter or larger. Lightweight navigation motion now paints at roughly 30 FPS and advances from elapsed time, while CRT texture remains on its quieter 10 FPS cadence and hidden/reduced-motion overlays automatically throttle themselves. Existing installations using the previous 40 ms default migrate to 33 ms automatically, while custom cadence values remain untouched. The indicator now has a subtle borderless operational core orbit and continuous state-dependent parallax buses: supercruise flows forward, flight and fighter counter-flow, exploration turns more slowly, scanners combine acquisition gates with a resolve sweep, and arrival/cooldown carry staggered secondary echoes. The separate current-route strip stays calm during travel; a live FSD/carrier arrival now confirms the cyan CURRENT origin, sends a single handoff through only the first upcoming leg and pulses that next full pip or dense tick, leaving every later waypoint static and unambiguous. FSD charging draws layered energy packets inward around a compressing aperture, then journal/Flags2-confirmed high-wake hyperspace reverses into faster opposing tunnel streaks and a restrained centre pulse before arrival; the generic Status jump bit used during an in-system low wake correctly remains SUPERCRUISE.
*   Reworked **Survey Operations** into a dedicated system-survey display without changing its established font scale or repeating a second system-mode title. It appears from active FSS intake, retains each resolved body through DSS assessment and records discovered asteroid belt clusters separately without corrupting star/planet completion totals. A focused-body card combines DSS, biological, geological, first-footfall, gravity and value evidence; live samples use clear three-stage pips; predicted, detected, sampling and completed organisms have explicit states; and geology-only bodies receive the same focused treatment. Completed biology moves into a compact full-species manifest, while live signal-cache fallback preserves body names and signals as detailed scans catch up without unnecessary geometry churn.
