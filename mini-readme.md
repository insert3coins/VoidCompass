# VoidCompass // UPDATE LOG

## v5.3.6 // Exploration-First Console
**Release Date:** 2026-Aug-09

*   Reduced the primary navigation rail from fourteen destinations to seven exploration-focused workspaces plus Settings: Dashboard, Explore & Survey, Galactic Atlas, Analytics, Profile, Field Tools and About.
*   Consolidated **Expedition Command** into the live, profile-aware Expedition page inside Explore & Survey instead of retaining a duplicate rail destination and launcher.
*   Replaced broad Operations with **Explorer Field Tools** for Ground/Exobiology, Mining, exploration Engineering/Synthesis, Colonisation Recon and focused Achievements; Combat/AX, Powerplay and Squadron/BGS no longer appear in the application shell.
*   Kept Mining as the single intentional side activity, including its journal-driven run history, refinery economics, core tracking and first-class Prospector Analysis overlay.
*   Retired the Compass cockpit companion, personas, cognition and all TTS/Piper callouts so the exploration console remains quiet, focused and free of background speech work.
*   Narrowed the Adaptive Command Deck to General Flight, Exploration, Mining, Surface Survey, Carrier Expedition and Station/Data Sale, removing unrelated dashboard queues and manual modes.
*   Removed Voice and Compass settings, onboarding choices, Dashboard cards, live-feed filters and runtime startup/shutdown hooks; native overlays, toasts, the Flight Log and journal heartbeat remain.
*   Refocused Achievements on exploration, travel, routes, exobiology, SRV, Carrier, Colonisation and Mining packs, and retired Colony Logistics from the normal overlay catalogue without deleting prior state.
*   Kept profile-aware themes, commander history, cargo/station/engineering-material support, EDSM and the deliberate visited-market EDDN uploader; no commander data was deleted.
*   Added a journal-backed **Bio Field Assistant** with ranked unfinished targets, active 1/3–3/3 sample guidance, colony spacing, terrain hints, value ranges and first-footfall context.
*   Added explainable per-body and per-system **Discovery Significance** ratings for prioritising measured discoveries without interruptive speech.
*   Added an embedded **Exploration Field Computer** for return-to-base distance/jump/time estimates, unsold-data exposure, verified sale services and route-endurance evidence covering fuel, hull, FSD, AFMU, heat sinks, SRV and jumponium.
*   Added profile-local **Expedition Sector Surveys** with bounded grid progress and a lightweight Atlas layer that preserves surveyed/incomplete cells while sampling untouched cells for smooth map control.
*   Added seven Mission Control goal templates: Galactic Region Passport, Valuable Worlds Survey, Odyssey Genus Collection, Local Sector Survey, Codex Fieldwork, Photo Chronicle and Deep Survey.
*   Expanded the Ground tool into a temporary **Surface Survey Trail** that begins at touchdown, records walking/SRV travel and bio samples, and guides the commander back to the ship without persisting stale trips across profiles or systems.
*   Rebuilt **Expedition Overview** as a live command centre with goal progress, survey yield, current-leg route tracking, a lightweight route strip, unsold-data exposure, return planning, ship readiness, bookmarks, recent activity and direct mission actions.
