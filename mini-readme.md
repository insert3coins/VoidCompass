# VoidCompass // UPDATE LOG

## v5.6.4 // Company in the Black
**Release Date:** 25 OCT 3312 *(Galactic Standard Time)*

* **Your Hatchling has a life of its own.** In its pod and in its overlay, it wanders about, yawns and dozes when tired, grumbles at an empty dish, sulks under a raincloud in a messy hold, sneezes when ill, stargazes, chases motes and waves at you. A larva hums, a Remlok bot sweeps its scanner, a sample sways and puffs spores. And it plays games by itself: keepy-uppy with a limpet, a canister tower it stacks until it topples, tag with a Thargon, and joining up stars into a constellation of its own.
  * The overlay gives it a strip of floor to move about on, with its name and mood underneath.
* **It lives through your flying with you.** In its pod and its overlay it acts out what you're doing as it happens: it braces for a hyperspace jump and comes out woozy, presses up to the glass when you dock, waves you off, cheers a touchdown, shouts WHEEE into supercruise, hides when the shields drop (or squares up, if it's brave), cheers a first discovery, sweats in the heat, worries when fuel runs low, and puts its sunglasses on while you scoop. Stars stream past in supercruise and station lights show through the glass while docked.
* **Toys from your trips.** Mining, a Guardian site, a Thargoid signal, a sampled organism, a first-discovered star or a station you've never docked at can leave it a keepsake in its pod (a chunk of Painite, a Guardian relic, a pebble, a beacon, a model station…), and it plays with them on its own. A larva kicks Guardian things away; anyone else is nervous of a Thargoid sensor.
* **A personality, favourites and tricks.** How you raise it makes it curious, brave, lazy, greedy or clingy, and that shapes what it chooses to do. It has a favourite food and game, and learns tricks from what you do together (spin, flip, wave, juggle, bow) and shows them off unasked. All shown under its name.
* **Dreams, your hours and your music.** Asleep, it dreams of the systems you visited today; it's sleepier late at night on your clock and says good morning; and when your music is playing it dances.
* **SNAP** on its page makes a postcard of its pod. The game page also gives it little noises of its own (a larva hums, a bot beeps), and the Watcher now notices it: its toys, its tricks, its frights and growing up.
* **The Hatchling overlay, Pet only.** Overlay Studio › Hatchling › Look: the panel as before, or just the pet, roaming a see-through strip anywhere on your screen. It shows what it wants in a bubble (or its needs as little bars beside it, or nothing), with its words and dreams over its head. Choose its size, how far it roams, its glow, and whether it chirps while you fly.
* **It knows where you are.** On a planet it hops by the world's real gravity (short and hard on a heavy world, slow and floaty on a light one) and shivers on icy worlds or sweats on hot ones. It rides along in the SRV and enjoys the bumps, waits at the glass while you're out on foot, and is glad when you're back aboard.
* **Welcome back.** Open Void Compass after a few hours away and it greets you, with what it got up to meanwhile: its naps, a game it played, whether it got hungry, and that it missed you.
* **Its day in the Captain's Log.** Each flight log ends with its own short diary for that session: toys found, tricks learned, frights, growing up.
* **Outfits it earns.** A scanner visor (500 jumps), a mining helmet (100 refining runs), a Guardian charm (3 Guardian finds), an explorer's scarf (50 first discoveries) and a bow tie (30 treats). WEAR on its page puts one on; they show in its overlay too.
* **It and the Watcher notice each other.** When the Watcher speaks, the pet turns to look its way; when the Watcher is in its weary mood, it goes and sits quietly near it.
* **It chatters (a little).** A few words of its own now and then, in a speech bubble on its page and its overlay: "Ooh. Different light." when you arrive, "Can I come out?" at a station, "We found it first!" on a first discovery, a word about its favourite toy or food, a dream it half remembers. Each kind talks its own way (a larva hums, a bot beeps and clips its words, a sample speaks soft and slow), its personality picks some of what it says, and with sounds on it babbles in little chirps. It never talks over the Watcher; once the Watcher has finished, it sometimes answers ("…yes."), and now and then the Watcher picks up on something it said. Overlay Studio › Hatchling › Chatter: Off, Quiet (the default) or Chatty.
* **More of your day reaches it.** It cheers your bounties and combat bonds (a larva mourns a Thargoid), is proud of completed missions and sorry about failed ones, throws a confetti party for a promotion, has a good look round a new ship (and knows it by your name for it), watches credits rain down when you sell data, loves an engineering upgrade (a bot most of all), sighs with relief at repairs and escaped interdictions, poses for your screenshots, gasps at a crime, huffs when docking is refused, waves off your fighter, and cheers finding every world in a system or a perfectly efficient surface map. Each one has its own few words, too.
* **Start again.** RESET on its page (press twice) lets your Hatchling go, eggs and hibernating ones too, and you can choose a new egg. It's remembered.
* **It dances to your music, its way.** It listens to what's playing (the same levels the music visualizers use) and dances to the beat it hears: it bounces to a heavy beat, headbangs to loud, bright music (a Remlok bot does the robot), shuffles to the middle of the road, sways with its eyes closed to something slow and quiet, and does a little jump and wiggle when the music swells. It dances a set when a track starts and now and then after, and in between it carries on with its own things, bobbing to the beat. On its page and in its overlay, and now and then it'll tell you it likes this one.
* **It comes along on your expeditions.** Fly an expedition and your Hatchling comes too ("ON THE COLONIA RUN"), keeping its own diary of the trip. Finish it together and it earns a badge, "Travelled with you to Colonia", with a party to celebrate. The expedition's page shows it aboard with its diary, and the trip's Void Wrapped has a card of its own: Along for the ride. On the way it'll ask whether you're nearly there.
* **Quicker start with the game running.** The journal catch-up at startup no longer waits a second between its small batches, so with a long session's journal the overlays come up in seconds instead of after an 11-second wait.
* **Shutdown is timed.** Each close writes how long every step took to logs/shutdown.log (the last 20 closes), so a slow close can be tracked down.
* **Lag fixed.** With music playing, the Hatchling overlay kept starting new requests for the music's levels and swamped the overlay server, which slowed every overlay (the Watcher's words came late, the Nav HUD was slow to update). It now listens once at a time, draws at most 30 frames a second whatever All overlays is set to, saves at most every few seconds, and treats a burst of scans as one moment.
* **Expeditions:** completing or deleting an expedition now takes its route off the Galaxy Minimap and the Nav HUD (it kept showing the trip). Your own waypoints stay.
