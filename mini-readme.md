# VoidCompass // UPDATE LOG

## v5.5.12 // Mining, Rebuilt
**Release Date:** 2026-Oct-18

* **The Mining page, rebuilt from the ground up** for both kinds of mining, in four tabs:
  * **LIVE RUN** changes with what you're doing. In a ring: the rock you just prospected (MINE, OPTIONAL, SKIP or CRACK IT against your target and minimum, with every material's share), the run so far (time, tonnes and tonnes an hour, rocks and how many had your target, limpets left, how long until the hold is full, and roughly what it's worth at the prices you've been getting), a chart of your last rocks against your minimum, and what you've refined. In the Rhino: your rigs on this planet (how far, which way, how long down, the order to collect them), the Rhino's hold and your ship's, what this kind of ground tends to hold and what it sells for, and the sites you've saved here.
  * **FIND** looks for rings by mineral and ring type (your own scans, then Spansh), and now **Rhino ground**: pick a material to see which grounds it turns up on, then find landable planets of that ground near you, with gravity and arrival distance limits. Your scanned rings, planets with mining sites and saved rings are kept here too. Each search has a CLEAR for when you're done with it (SELL's buyers too).
  * **SELL** lists what's in your holds with what it's likely worth, finds the best payers for any of it within your Sell cargo distance, and shows every mined material you've sold: how much, your average, best and last price.
  * **RECORD** is all your mining, read from your journals: tonnes refined in the ship and hauled in the Rhino, mining income, rocks prospected (high, medium, low), motherlodes, limpets, days mining; tonnes and income month by month; what you've mined most, where, your richest rock of each material, and your recent runs.
* **The Watcher knows what you took out of the ship.** It used to call everything "the SRV" (and once or twice the wrong one). Now the Scarab, the Scorpion, the Rhino, the Nomad and a fighter each get their own words as you launch them and while you're out in them; loading in while already in an SRV no longer mistakes it for your ship.
* **More for the Watcher to say about mining:** a rich rock as you prospect it, a motherlode, a cracked asteroid, the Rhino's hold or your ship's filling up mid-run, drifting in a ring, and more lines for refining in the ship and in the Rhino.
* **The Watcher notices a full hold**, once a load: the Rhino's, the Scarab's and Scorpion's little ones, and your ship's, whether you filled it mining, trading or picking things up (not when it's just sitting full). Restarting Void Compass mid-session no longer leaves it unsure which vehicle you're in or how big your hold is.
* **Fixed: COPY NEXT (and every other copy button) sometimes put nothing on the clipboard.** If another program was holding the clipboard at that moment (clipboard history or a clipboard manager, Discord, OBS, remote desktop), the copy quietly failed. It now waits a moment and tries again, and the Event Feed says what was copied, or why it couldn't be.
* **Auto-copy the next waypoint is easy to find now:** a switch right under **COPY NEXT DESTINATION** on the Command Deck's Flight Corridor panel, AUTO-COPY NEXT WAYPOINT ON ARRIVAL. When it's on, each time you arrive in a system your next saved waypoint goes on the clipboard, ready to paste into the galaxy map. It's still in Settings › Flight (now called "Auto-copy the next waypoint") and on Explore & Survey's ROUTE tab.
* **Planetary operations, rebuilt as one page.** Materials & Sites and Ground & Exobio are now one Planetary operations page, in three tabs:
  * **ON THE GROUND**: the compass, with a big readout of how far and which way, coordinates to paste in, USE WHERE I AM, BACK TO THE SHIP and the cockpit popup switch; a new **surface map**: north up with distance rings and a scale, your trail from the ship, a dashed line to the compass target, numbered samples with the range each needs, Codex finds, your markers and saved sites, all labelled; hover for how far, click any of them to steer the compass there (or the ship to head back); FIT ALL, AROUND ME and zoom. And the planet itself, with its raw materials.
  * **EXOBIOLOGY**: the planet's biology as Survey Operations reads it: what you've done, what you're sampling, and what could be here, each with its value (first footfall's ×5 included) and Codex flags. While you sample, how far you are from your last sample and how much further to go, on a map with each sample's range. Every planet with biology in the system, unfinished and richest first. And your unsold biology, ready for Vista Genomics.
  * **SITES**: your saved surface sites, editable in place, with SEND TO COMPASS; ADD A SITE opens the form only when you want it (SAVE THIS SPOT on the ground fills it in). The heat map and by-material views are here too.
  * Opening the old Ground & Exobio (from the Explore page's GROUND NAV, say) lands on ON THE GROUND. What the ground tends to hold for the Rhino now lives on the Mining page only.
* **Fixed: the Watcher said the Rhino had 0% fuel.** Out in an SRV, the game reports the ship's tank as empty and the SRV's fuel separately; the Watcher read the ship's. Void Compass now learns each vehicle's tank (the Rhino, Scarab, Scorpion and Nomad) from a full one and tracks its fuel as a percentage: the Watcher warns at 25% with words for the vehicle (head back to the ship, not "find a star"), the Navigation HUD's gauge shows the percentage, and the Mining page's Rhino view has a FUEL tile. On the Command Deck, FUEL INTEGRITY shows the vehicle's tank while you're in one, and the departure checks add a row for it. Logging in while already out in an SRV no longer sets your ship's fuel to 0%.
* **New in the Local API:** `/mining/record`, the whole record.
