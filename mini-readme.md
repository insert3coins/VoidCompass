# VoidCompass // UPDATE LOG

## v5.7.8 // The Wider View
**Release Date:** 13 NOV 3312 *(Galactic Standard Time)*

* **Explore & Survey, rebuilt.** Seven tabs: OVERVIEW, SYSTEM, SEARCH, FIRSTS, CHECKLIST, SYSTEM MAP and ARCHIVE, on the whole width of the window. The survey of the system you're in heads every tab (what it's worth and what's new there, with the Directive under it; its name and next jump are in the top bar), every list is a card as tall as what's in it, stars and worlds show their colours, values carry a bar, biology shows as chips, and an empty list says so in one line. SEARCH has its filters down the left, and FIRSTS shows your records as tiles. Every table sorts by any column, folds to fit narrow windows and copies as CSV.
* **Nearby.** Every known system round you (or round a system you choose), from Spansh: who's been there, its main star, body count, notable worlds, value and how far it is in jumps. Filters, bookmarks, boxel mode, and a codex pill where a body type would be new to your Codex for the region. It sits beside the body table of the system you're viewing (that on the left, Nearby on the right), side by side or stacked, with the route ahead at the top.
* **The system's body table.** Your own scans laid over Spansh's: class, arrival distance, gravity, biology and geology (with what each species is worth and which colours you're missing), rings and hotspots, firsts and your records, value now and fully mapped, potential green gas giants, and the order to scan them in. Open a body for its details, the Guardian sites and geology other commanders reported there, and a pin for the Planetary compass.
* **The route ahead.** The next systems on your plotted route, each with who's been there and what's worth stopping for.
* **Search.** Systems, bodies and stations by what's in them, from your own journals or Spansh, and the nearest station with a service you need.
* **Route page.** Plot Route has its own page on the deck rail, laid out like Explore & Survey (the plotter, the route and the dock finder as cards, stars in their colours), with the nearest place to dock beside the route (above it on a narrow window), so a long route never hides it. Each stop in the list has its own copy button (the next stop's says NEXT), and a stop ahead can be marked done to skip it: the Nav HUD, COPY NEXT, the top bar and the maps go on to the one after; AUTO-COPY NEXT beside the plotter puts each next stop on the clipboard as you arrive (the same switch as in Settings); a click on the route in the top bar opens it. Spansh's neutron, exact, road to riches, exobiology, Earth-like, ammonia, terraformable and tourist routes from the deck. The route is followed as you jump: stops ticked off, the next one targeted for you to copy, permits flagged, fuel warnings, jumps and light years left, and your pace. A plotted route becomes your waypoint route, so the Nav HUD, COPY NEXT, the copy on arrival, the Galaxy Minimap and the Galactic Atlas all follow it; the waypoints you had come back when you clear it. Plot Route is the source: clearing the route in Expeditions or the waypoint list clears it everywhere, and with no route in the game the top bar's NEXT STOP follows it.
* **Controls that match.** Checkboxes, dropdowns, number boxes and sliders follow your theme everywhere (no white browser squares or spinners), with one focus ring, and choices like TABLE/QUEUE, LIST/TREE/SCHEMATIC or the route plotters are one joined control with the chosen one lit.
* **One plotter.** Expeditions plots its legs and its "route to" on the same plotter, and a leg you fly shows on the Route page.
* **Firsts.** Unsold data and what you'd leave behind by jumping now, your records (hottest star, heaviest landable, fastest spin, widest ring, farthest from Sol) and a firsts watch.
* **Checklist.** The Codex's organics for the region you're in, species by species and colour by colour.
* **Journal Log.** In ARCHIVE: every journal event as one readable line, searchable, with the raw event a tap away.
* **Flight header.** Current system, flight state, region and the next jump lead the deck, with a compact row for distance from Sol, jump range, fuel, data aboard and the carrier. FLIGHT DETAILS opens the full readings and service status; overlay switches open from PANELS.
* **CMDR tabs.** Money and ranks (with an estimate to the next exobiology rank), every ship and stored module with where it is and the cost to bring it here, EDSY and Coriolis links for every ship, missions soonest first with massacre kills, sessions, vouchers held (lost if you die), and earnings by kind and month with your best stations and species.
* **History.** Explore & Survey's second tab, in place of Scout & Recon, the Value Ledger and the Captain's Log: a row per session, trips from sale to sale (what each paid, credits per hour and per jump), your top 25 finds and records, the trip as Markdown for Discord or Reddit, CSV exports, and your EDSM flight log brought in on request.
* **The Dashboard is the ship's bridge.** It follows what you're doing (exploring, mining, trading, fighting, on foot or docked) and leads with the panels for it. Your crew on watch and their orders sit beside it, and a panel only shows when there's something in it. It uses the whole window, laid out on purpose: where you are, your route and the ship across the top, then what's under way, the session beside your priorities and the region's Codex, and the live feed along the bottom. The Mission Directive is left to Explore & Survey and the departure checks to the status line. The log window stays at the bottom. Settings has a start page: Dashboard or Explore & Survey.
* **Survey overlay, rebuilt around Explore & Survey.** Its system view reads the same body table as Explore's SYSTEM tab: a WORLDS list in the suggested scan order: each world on a line of its own (first discovered, first footfall, new to your Codex, green gas giants, worth now and max, marked YOURS or SPANSH), with its species listed under its name, one a line (analysed, being sampled or detected, colour, Codex flag, value), then its geology. The latest scan is a highlighted row tagged NEW rather than a card of its own. Nothing pages: a big system makes the overlay taller (up to 900 px, or the room below it on its screen), then its least pressing worlds fold to one line, then to chips; the world you just scanned or are sampling always keeps its full row. Overlay Studio sets how tall it grows ("Grow up to", 500 to 1,200 px), whether species are listed under each world, and whether quiet worlds are listed; the old rotation settings are gone, since nothing rotates. Worlds other commanders reported show before you scan them, dashed and marked SPANSH (Overlay Studio's "Spansh bodies" switch turns that off; it asks Spansh once per system). The header carries the system's worth and how many worlds are yours and Spansh's. The sampling card and the planet view are as they were. The overlay is a little wider (480).
* **Better biology guesses.** What could live on a world now comes from Starling's species rules (Daddyg88's COVAS, from EDEXO's data, used with permission): on our own journals they listed every species really analysed, where the old rules missed a fifth. The old rules still pick the likeliest, which lead the lists and set the estimates; the rest show as "Also possible". A bug that judged air pressure in pascals instead of atmospheres (and hid about a third of the species found) is fixed, and saved scans are worked out again. Concha Biconcavis is priced right (19M; it carried a placeholder), Radicoida Unica is spelt right, and the Thargoid coral is priced, all checked against the game's own Codex rewards; and one-of-a-kind finds (Radicoida Unica, the Void Heart) are never guessed or counted.
* **Unsold firsts since your last sale.** FIRSTS (and History's "since your last sale") read the sale's time properly: before, every first you'd ever found stayed on the list.
* **Smoother deck.** Explore's Archive and the Galactic Atlas read your scan history in the background instead of freezing the deck for a second or more on a long history, and the System Map no longer reopens your saved sites or scans the whole hotspot list on every refresh. Hidden dashboard panels stay idle, unchanged readings keep their existing elements, and Explore avoids rebuilding hidden queues and archive pages. Checklists open while sample history loads separately (read in the background after start-up), with cached lists for quick tab changes.
* **Survey overlay.** Biology first, then the rest; quieter when a system has nothing to say.
* **Smaller things.** A remark you can leave on any system, said when you target it again; a word before you jump away from a lot of unscanned value; potential green gas giants; where your landing pad is when docking is granted; your fuel scoop rate; crew warnings when you're wanted, in the wrong suit, low on oxygen or out of limpets; a mining run summed up when it ends; an optional auto honk on arrival (Settings, off by default); and, from Canonn's reports, the phenomena other commanders found in space in a system you arrive in, when they're not yet in your Codex for the region (Settings › Crew calls).
* **Geology and Phenomena checklists.** CHECKLIST now has ORGANICS, GEOLOGY and PHENOMENA: Canonn's reported sites by region against your Codex, and 📷 opens Canonn's picture of any species, formation or phenomenon (loaded only then, credited to the commander who took it).
* **Species names said right.** The crew's voices say the exobiology Latin properly ("Fonticulua", "Clypeus Lacrimam")
* **Target sounds.** A fanfare when you target a system nobody has reported, an upbeat pair for one known but unscanned by you, a thud for one you've been to (Settings › Crew calls).
* **Crew calls.** "Sell here" when you dock somewhere that buys the data you carry; a warning when you land elsewhere with a sample run unfinished, and word of a run a new species just discarded; modules the AFMU left switched off after a repair.
* **Where to cash vouchers.** Beside your bounties and bonds on the CMDR tab: each faction's nearest station, and the nearest Interstellar Factors.
* **The Nav HUD's system lines.** The distance from Sol or your home system (Overlay Studio), and your standing with whoever controls a populated system ("Allied with Mother Gaia", red when Hostile).
* **Sell all.** Trading's Sell search ranks the stations that pay most for your whole hold, and says what each one doesn't buy.
* **Pop out.** Any page but the Dashboard, the Galactic Atlas too, opens in a window of its own for a second monitor, straight onto the page, and each page's window comes back where you last put it. Popouts use the full window for their page, without the main header or status bars.
* **Bind auto honk.** Settings → Flight & exploration has a recorder for your Discovery Scanner's fire key, including a bare key or a keyboard combination. Auto honk holds it after a hyperspace arrival, cancels stale arrivals and releases it if you leave the game or open a menu.
* **Mute a line.** 🔇 beside any line in a companion's thoughts and it's never said again; SETUP brings it back. Answers to you and the story are never muted.
* **New Local API routes.** `/explore/nearby`, `/explore/system`, `/explore/route`, `/journal/log`, `/commander/earnings` and `/history`.
* **Journal fixes.** A Market.json from another station no longer becomes this one's market; legacy (pre-Odyssey) and beta journals are never uploaded to EDDN or EDSM; carrier fuel no longer counts reserved space; the music mode no longer sticks after leaving the galaxy map.
* **Overlays can hide while the game is closed.** A new Overlay Studio switch, Hide with the game closed, puts every overlay but the music player away when Elite shuts down (or crashes) and brings them back when you load in.
* **No ×5 in populated systems.** Exobiology estimates no longer count the first-footfall bonus where people live: the game doesn't pay it there.
