# VoidCompass // UPDATE LOG

## v5.7.7 // Orders from the Bridge
**Release Date:** TBA 3312 *(Galactic Standard Time)*

* **Crew Orders: the crew asks things of you.** Until now your companions talked about what you'd done. Now they give you orders. Every order rests on a real fact (your journal, Spansh, your Codex, your trades) and says where that fact came from and when. The journal finishes each one: you never tick anything off. The officer who gave it follows it through, cheers the halfway mark and debriefs you at the end.
* **Each officer orders what they care about.** Lyra hunts species and colours missing from your Codex ("Two Bacterium you've never logged within 180 ly") and wants samples cashed in before something goes wrong. Axiom wants worlds mapped, systems finished in the FSS, and first discoveries out in the black. Meridian finds neutron routes that save you hours, regions you've never entered and new distance records. Aster finds better prices for what's in your hold, sells charts and samples sensibly, and keeps your construction sites and Powerplay merits supplied. The Watcher has long orders to far places (Sagittarius A*, Colonia, Beagle Point) and odd little errands: sit ten minutes in a system nobody has seen, take a picture of something it would like, walk where nobody has walked.
* **Campaigns.** Seven of them, in stages handed between officers, with targets taken from your own record so no two commanders get the same one: Every Colour, The Long Haul, A Clean Sheet, The Ledger, First Light, Home and The Fork. Each opens and closes with a scene, a line from each officer in turn, in their own voices. In The Fork, Meridian and Axiom disagree about what to do next and you settle it.
* **Your record with each officer.** Every order you finish raises your standing with the officer who gave it, from New to the watch up to Indispensable. Each officer has an Order File, a short side story of five passages unlocked by the orders you do for them rather than by hours flown. Seventeen commendations to earn, from First Orders to each officer's Right Hand.
* **Orders on the companion page.** A new ORDERS tab beside BRIDGE, with a badge when an offer is waiting: offers waiting for your answer (with WHY: every fact and its source), the orders you're on with their progress, the campaign with its stages and scenes, the SERVICE RECORD (the five officers in a roster, each with progress to their next standing, and the one you pick in full: their numbers and their Order File; commendations below, with each officer's Right Hand in a row of its own), and a log. ASK FOR ORDERS asks the crew right now.
* **The Crew Orders overlay.** Every order in the cockpit at once: those waiting for your answer on top, then the campaign, then the rest, each in its officer's colour with its open objectives, progress and how far the target is. The accept and decline hotkeys show on the newest offer, the one they answer. It shows only while there is an order.
* **Answer without leaving the cockpit.** Two new hotkeys accept or decline the waiting order. In TALK, ask "any orders?", "what are my orders", "accept the order", "decline it", "cancel the order", "who gave me this order" or "how far is the target". Accepting copies the target system, ready for the galaxy map, and can open the galaxy map for you. The Local API has /orders routes for a Stream Deck.
* **The crew talk to each other.** At the moments that matter (low fuel, a full scoop, a long jump run, heat, a narrow escape, launch, a new distance record, black holes and neutron stars, a first discovery, a world worth mapping, the Codex, a species sampled, landing, a first footfall, data sold, docking, trade, mining, a finished order, a lost ship, a long session) and in quiet stretches, the companion on watch and a crewmate or two have a short exchange, each in their own voice, each stepping into the overlay as they speak, each line waiting for the last to finish. Over 300 exchanges, every pair of the five covered, and none heard again until the rest of its moment has been. Only companions whose voice is on take part. On the companion page, under Its thoughts: Crew banter (off, now and then, often), at most one every 10 to 60 minutes, two or up to three of them, Length (Short, a line and its answer, by default; or Full, the whole exchange), and when they talk (in flight, exploring, in port, crew moments, quiet stretches). Campaign scenes now take turns the same way.
* **The crewmate who speaks, shows.** When a companion who isn't on watch speaks (an order, a remark, a debrief line), their own display steps into the overlay while they talk (Lyra's face, Axiom's crystal, Meridian's compass, Aster's halo, the Watcher's eye) in their own colour and with their own service pips, then fades back to whoever has the watch. The display stays the same size. An order's heading also names them: "NEW ORDER · AXIOM".
* **Signing off for the time of day.** When the game shuts down, your companions say goodbye for your clock instead of always "Goodnight": "Have a good morning", "Enjoy your afternoon", "Have a good evening", goodnight late at night, and "Get some sleep" in the small hours. "Tonight" becomes "today" or "this evening" too.
* **Voice is optional.** Orders never depend on it: every line shows as a written thought with the officer's name, and the page and overlay carry everything. An officer off watch still speaks only in its own voice, so when its voice is off or not downloaded, the companion on watch passes the order on in its own words.
* **Never a nag.** One offer a session by default, or now and then, or often (every hour at most), or only when you ask. Never in a fight, a menu or a jump, or in a session's first minutes. Turn down the same kind of order twice and that officer stops offering it, and says so. Choose which kinds you want, and turn campaigns or the Watcher's long orders off, in Settings › Flight & exploration › Crew Orders.
* **Orders everywhere else.** The order you've been on longest is a cue in the Mission Directive. A finished order goes in the Captain's Log, the session's flight log and the Flight Recorder, a commendation pops up where achievements do, and Void Wrapped has a new Orders card for your year.
* **Orders on your maps.** The Navigation HUD's Survey and Planet pages show the order that fits them ("ORDER · LYRA · THE TEAL CERBRUS · 14 LY"). The Galaxy Minimap puts a small flag, in the officer's colour, where each order points (hollow while it waits for your answer; a new Crew orders switch in Overlay Studio), and the Galactic Atlas has a Crew orders layer.
* **Overtaken.** Mapping orders are about first maps. If a scan shows another commander mapped your target first, it drops out of the order; if that was the last of it, the officer takes it with good grace (no mark against you) and offers the next target.
* **Rewards you can see.** Service pips on each companion's bezel, one for every standing past New to the watch, gold at Indispensable (the displays stay the same size). Every campaign ribbon, Whole Crew and each officer's Right Hand also unlocks a postcard theme of its own, added to your themes. And a few hours after each campaign, in a quiet moment, the crew looks back on it together; those talks are kept in the companion page's ORDERS tab.
* **Said right.** Sagittarius A* is now "A-Star", not "A asterisk", and Concha is "Conka". The other new names were checked against the voices' own phonemes.
* **Trade Route and Crew Orders overlays fit at any text size.** They fit themselves to their window like the Cargo, Prospector, Planet Materials and Station overlays: long station names, commodities and objectives wrap instead of being cut off with "…", and nothing falls off the bottom at 150% or 200% text, or on a PC whose Windows scaling draws the page bigger than its window.
* **No more "map it" for a world you've just mapped.** The game scans a world again as your probes finish, and your companions took that as a new world worth mapping. Now a world mapped by you, or by anyone before you, gets no "map it", and each world is remarked on once a session.
* **Said once.** When the watch changes hands (the rota does it often while you explore), the companion taking over now carries on from the last: it knows the system, its survey and first maps, the worlds and materials already remarked on, the route and where you are, instead of saying it all again. And idle talk about where you are (the star, the way home, the world you're on, the station or ring) comes once a visit, not every few minutes.
* **Home is home.** Docking anywhere used to bring "Back home" and "Home station" lines. Now those wait for your home system (the one set in Settings, or Sol); anywhere else, the Watcher says its usual docking lines, and Aster, Axiom, Meridian and Lyra each have new words of their own for docking anywhere, often naming the station.
* **Asked to scan only where it's worth it.** Your companions remember a system's traffic: where nobody comes through they'll still say "honk when ready", but in a system with more than five visits a year (from EDSM, usually known before you arrive because Jump Info asks as you target it) they just say hello. Axiom's "finish the FSS" order follows the same rule.
* **A steadier watch rota.** The companion page now says what the rota is doing ("Rota: docked, Aster takes over in 8 s", "Holding your pick until 23:51") with a button to let the rota decide now. Docking hands over within ten seconds, other duties settle in their own time, and the gap between changes is down from three minutes to ninety seconds. Each decision is written to the debug log.
