# VoidCompass // UPDATE LOG

## v5.7.6 // TBA
**Release Date:** TBA 3312 *(Galactic Standard Time)*

* **A watch rota (optional).** Turn on Watch rota on the companion page (under Its thoughts) and the crew changes the watch with what you're doing, within about a minute of it starting. You choose who takes each duty: docked (Aster by default), scanning in the FSS or DSS (Axiom), on a planet's surface on foot or in an SRV (Axiom), on a plotted route (Meridian) deep space, from 1,000 light years out by default, or 250 to 10,000 as you choose (the Watcher), and ordinary flight with none of those (nobody by default); or "Whoever's on watch" to leave a duty alone. The rota waits at least three minutes between changes, choosing a companion yourself holds your choice for half an hour, and every change is a proper handover, by name. Off by default, saved per profile.
* **The crew sees you off.** When an expedition sets out, the companion on watch marks the start and each crewmate you've flown with adds a line of their own, one after another, off watch: Meridian on the route and fuel, Axiom on what's worth scanning, Aster on the paperwork, the Watcher in its own way.
* **Captain's Log postscripts.** When more than one companion stood a watch in a session, the flight log is written by the one on watch and signed off with a short postscript from each of the others.
* **A story for the whole crew.** Once all four companions have told how they came aboard, they begin a story together: a scene now and then in a quiet moment, at least two hours apart, a line from each of them in turn and in their own voices. It's shared, so whoever is on watch carries on where the last one stopped, and the STORY tab keeps every scene you've heard.
* **The crew in Void Wrapped.** A new card for your year (or your expedition): how many hours each companion stood watch and how many watches, the stories each one told, a line each of them actually said to you, and who stood the most of it.
* **Rhino survey.** Mark a survey's centre where you stand (Ctrl+Alt+7), drive out and mark its edge (Ctrl+Alt+8). The keys can be changed in Settings › Hotkeys, and the Mining page has buttons for both. Void Compass then shades everything your SRV's scanner has reached inside the circle (2 km round you), gives the coverage as a percentage, and lays out sweep stops whose scanner circles cover the whole area, pointing you to the nearest one not yet swept. Coverage is kept per planet, so it survives a restart. Your companion says when the survey is set, half covered and done.
* **The best patch.** The Rhino map now rings the richest patch so far: refining pulses gathered within 150 m of each other, counted. Elite gives no deposit positions, so it's only ever where you stood for each pulse. Pulses are now kept per planet too. Your companion says when a best patch first shows and when another takes the lead.
* **A rig pattern.** After your first rig (or round the best patch, before any rig is down), the Rhino map shows a hex pattern at the rig spacing (78 m) for the rigs still to place, and RIG HERE points to the nearest spot in it. A spot far from your other rigs is flagged.
* **On every Rhino screen.** The survey's coverage, sweep stops and next stop, the best patch and the rig pattern are on the Rhino map; the Rig Tracker shows the survey %, the next stop, the best patch and the next rig spot with distance and direction; and the Mining page has a RHINO SURVEY panel with all of it.
* **TALK knows where things are.** Ask "where's the nearest material trader?" (or raw, manufactured or encoded trader, Interstellar Factors, technology broker, shipyard, outfitting, black market, Universal Cartographics, Vista Genomics, refuel, repair, rearm, Search and Rescue, Pioneer Supplies, Apex, Frontline Solutions, bartender, refinery contact, redemption office, or just a station) and your companion searches Spansh from where you are: the three nearest, how far, how far in from the star and whether they have large pads. Fleet carriers are left out unless you ask for one. It uses the uplink, so trading searches must be on in Settings, under Integrations.
* **"How far is Colonia?"** Ask how far it is to any system. Your companion answers from your own travel history first, then from well-known places (Sol, Colonia, Beagle Point, Sagittarius A*) and your home, and only goes to Spansh, with trading searches on, for a system you've never been to. The answer includes about how many jumps it is at your jump range. Route questions ("how far to the next system?") still get the route.
* **Materials by grade.** "How much arsenic do I have?" now works without the word "materials", and says the grade and the limit ("Arsenic: 42 of 250 (raw, grade 2)"). "Materials by grade" and "grade 4 raw materials" give totals by grade, how many are full, and the most plentiful.
* **Community goals.** Ask "how are my community goals doing?" (or "any CGs?"): each goal you've joined, where it is, your contribution, your percentile band, the tier reached and the time left, kept from Elite's own CommunityGoal reports.
* **Officers who sum you up.** Aster, Axiom and Meridian have more to say about your habits (flying, scanning, cargo, combat, trade) and new lines about your style that read properly ("By my figures you are mostly an explorer, at 62 percent"); the old ones said things like "leans towards an explorer".
* **Docking, answered every time.** After you asked for docking (most often by voice or TALK, whose reply just spoke), your companion stayed silent when the station granted it: the grant waited behind the reply, could go unsaid on a quiet setting or on a busy docking day, and wasn't said twice within twenty minutes. Now the station's answer to your request is always said, aloud and in fresh words: granted (with your pad), denied (and why), cancelled, or timed out, the last two being new. A request made from the panel is announced too ("Requesting docking at Jameson Memorial"); one you asked for by voice isn't said twice.
* **Companions who notice more of your game.** We checked the journal schema event by event: your companions now speak to 55 more moments. Danger and the law: a cockpit breach, a Thargoid shutdown, self destruct, crimes you commit (in plain words, with the fine or bounty) and ones done to you, paying fines and bounties, an impounded ship released, reboot repairs, and pulling someone out of supercruise. Missions: accepted, failed, abandoned, redirected (go back for the pay), Search and Rescue handed in, and community goals joined, paid out and left. Ships: buying, selling and naming one, outfitting, transfers, engineers inviting you, unlocking and ranking up, new materials and material trades. Your carrier (bought, renamed, decommissioned or kept, tritium, trade orders), colonies (claimed, released, the beacon, deliveries) and Powerplay (joining, leaving, defecting, merits, salary). And the people and kit around you: suits, weapons and upgrades, dropship deployments, multicrew, hired pilots, and wing invites. All four companions, in their own words.
* **FOLLOW ME after a restart.** Before the journal has said where you are, the Galactic Atlas no longer treats 0, 0, 0 as your position, which could send FOLLOW ME to Sol until your next jump. FOLLOW ME itself was already kept between sessions.
* **Hotkeys in their groups.** Settings › Hotkeys no longer puts every other shortcut under OVERLAY CONTROLS. Two new groups, NAVIGATION (Navigation HUD pages, Galaxy Minimap view) and RHINO, RIGS AND SURVEY (the rig keys, Rhino Map range and the survey keys), join EXPEDITIONS AND COLONIES (mark this place, Construction Needs fold and refresh); OVERLAY CONTROLS keeps just show/hide all and the Layout Studio.
