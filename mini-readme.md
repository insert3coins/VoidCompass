# VoidCompass // UPDATE LOG

## v5.3.7.9 // Navigation State Instrument
**Release Date:** 2026-Aug-21

*   Rebuilt the Navigation HUD indicator as a restrained, fully code-native state instrument. A recognisable state sigil now occupies the left wing, the active state remains readable at the centre, and a state-specific live response occupies the right wing—adapting the useful ring/icon/readout idea from the concept mock-up without importing its large panel or duplicating existing telemetry.
*   Gave all 29 verified profiles a deliberate visual signature across four related cockpit dialects: flight and FSD flow, exploration and scanning, station and surface operations, and hazards. Docking, maps, vehicle hand-offs, mass lock, asteroid fields, Fleet Carrier transit and the full orbital/glide/surface lifecycle remain individually recognisable rather than sharing a generic animation.
*   Deepened the right-hand operational readout around the actual Elite state: FSS uses a tuning spectrum, DSS uses planetary probe coverage, Galaxy/System/Orrery/Power/Codex maps resolve differently, docked and landed states retain their pad or gear contacts, normal flight uses an attitude vector, SRV/Nomad use terrain contacts, mass lock shows a blocked gravity well, signals use a target waveform and interdiction uses a converging escape solution.
*   Made the complete left wing genuinely live instead of leaving a small sigil on an empty rail. Layered state-specific travel flow, scanner returns, docking cells, terrain response, vehicle hand-off, hazard convergence, FSD energy, Carrier power and planetary guidance now animate around the existing attitude, contact and suit details. Supercruise replaces its disconnected upper/lower half-circles with one continuous perspective flow of converging rails, accelerating streaks and a travelling compression front—without another standalone gate or icon.
*   Removed the legacy cross-instrument modifier layer that could leak landing, scoop or FSD details into unrelated states: fuel scooping now animates only beneath the fuel readout, while gravity and neutron readiness remain restrained and limited to relevant surface or flight sigils.
*   Consolidated the accumulated Navigation HUD implementations into one sustained-state renderer and one bounded journal-event overlay, deleting the retired V1/V2/V3 glyph, boot-rail and fallback motion chains. Fuel, refuel and Jet Cone events can no longer create a second green/accent pulse in the indicator.
*   Turned journal activity into one coordinated motion system: a bounded energy envelope now briefly deepens the sustained state, route/survey/resource events hand off visibly from the left wing to the right without crossing the native label, and scans, mining, hazards, docking and flight changes create restrained reactions within the active instrument rather than an unrelated generic flash.
*   Replaced the universal transition arcs with family-specific morphs for Supercruise, FSD charge/jump/arrival, scanning, docking, planetary flight, hazards, vehicle hand-offs and Carrier transit. Added lightweight theme-derived parallax and glow depth, with no raster loading or Canvas blur.
*   Added a profile-aware **Navigation animation** control with Calm, Standard and Energetic levels. Both Navigation HUD layouts retain the established 30 FPS budget, theme-aware colour depth and detailed reduced-motion state sigils; the abandoned raster concepts remain development artwork only and are not loaded or packaged by the application.
