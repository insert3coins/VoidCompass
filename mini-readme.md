# VoidCompass // UPDATE LOG

## v5.4.9.5 // Nav HUD Holograms, One-Screen Overlay Studio & Gravity Warning
**Release Date:** 2026-Sep-27

* Brought back an animated effect for every Navigation HUD state, rebuilt as a hologram: each of about 100 states has its own scene in a full-width display beside the ship, including tumbling asteroids, the hyperspace tunnel, the FSD charge core, a planet below in orbit, docking gates and SRV terrain.
* The ship's portrait now carries its own aura (supercruise streaks, charge rings, the ground rising on approach, the landing pad), and shows shield rings that break up red when shields drop, fuel-scoop plasma streaming into the nose, heat haze, the cargo scoop, hardpoints and a landing-gear pulse, all from Status.json.
* State changes fold the old scene to a line and open the new one from it, and journal events play short effects again (the discovery scan pulse, route plotted, neutron-star arrival jets, warnings, touchdown). Scenes use real data where it exists: the arrival star's class colour, FSS progress, DSS probes used, signal threat level, altitude and descent rate. A neutron or white-dwarf supercharge now charges the supercruise corridor blue, with 1–3 tier chevrons.
* The scene display grew from 38 to 52 px without enlarging the HUD window, and scenes keep round objects round, so dishes, scanner discs and orbits no longer look squashed. SIGNAL TARGET is now treated as a target rather than a signal lock, and FSD INJECTION shows in blue.
* Rebuilt Gravity Warning as a cockpit notice with a gauge against your gravity limit, the planet's name and system, and a live approach line with altitude and descent rate. It warns again once on final approach below 2.5 km and grows with overlay text size.
* Survey Operations now holds completely still when rotation is Off, or on Auto at or below its world count: every biology world is listed at once, and catalogue bodies that do not fit are counted instead of paged. Previously only the spotlight obeyed the setting, so a system of 9–15 worlds still cycled its list.
* Rebuilt Overlay Studio as one screen instead of two views. It shows one display at a time, chosen from a map of your monitors, with each overlay's switch in the surface list and its own settings beside the stage. Settings save as you change them; overlays can move to another display keeping their place, Smart Snap treats the seam between monitors as an edge, and arrow keys nudge the selected overlay.
* Fixed overlays arriving late or not at all after about five minutes, when Windows' background throttling made hidden overlays look dead and they were reloaded every 12 seconds. The dashboard now fetches a lost stylesheet again, and reloads once if its script fails to load, instead of starting half-styled or frozen.
* The logs now say more: host log lines carry the time, a failed dashboard request records Chromium's own error code, and errors logged anywhere in the app are kept in crash_report.log.
