# VoidCompass // UPDATE LOG

## v5.7.5 // Steady As She Goes
**Release Date:** 6 NOV 3312 *(Galactic Standard Time)*

* **Power distributor in every vehicle.** The Navigation HUD shows your SYS, ENG and WEP pips in every SRV and in a fighter, not just your ship, straight from Elite's Status.json. They stay hidden on foot and when you're a passenger or crew.
* **Companions that talk along your route.** Every companion now follows a plotted route: it notices a long route being plotted, the halfway point, five jumps to go, the last jump and arriving at the destination, and warns when a star on the way has no fuel to scoop (a neutron star, white dwarf, brown dwarf and so on). Meridian, the navigator, treats the route as its own business. Arrival remarks now name the star's class again: Elite reports it before the jump, not after.
* **A full scan, reported.** When the FSS has found every body, your companion tells you what the system holds, from the scans themselves: how many bodies, which are worth mapping (Earth-likes, water and ammonia worlds, terraformables), where there's biology and how many signals, and how many are first discoveries. All four companions do it in their own words, even on Occasional, and if it can't be said straight away it waits its turn instead of being lost.
* **More to say, about everything.** Aster, Axiom and Meridian have far more, and richer, lines across every kind of moment, in both commentary styles: SRVs (Scarab, Scorpion, Nomad, Rhino), fighters, landing, walking, taxis, mining and the Rhino, prospecting, samples, Codex entries, first discoveries, notable worlds and full scans; combat, hull and shields, interdictions, combat zones, dropships and losses; neutron boosts, new regions, revisits, records and distance from home; docking, missions, sales, achievements, promotions, your power, squadron and carrier; every stage of an expedition; and the quiet hours (night and morning, music, Galnet, your ship, long sessions and how long you've flown together). Every topic now has at least nine lines, four of them in the plainer style, so Restrained, Precise and Plain no longer sound clipped.
* **Companions that know the Rhino and its rigs.** Refining in the Rhino is the rigs' yield, and Aster, Axiom and Meridian now say so in their own words ("Rig yield: Platinum. Into the Rhino's hold it goes."); before, they talked as if you'd picked materials up by hand. Rigs aren't in Elite's journal, so the rig hotkeys now tell your companion too: a rig placed, a rig that has sat its two minutes with you beside it, each one collected and the last one home, a rig getting too far away, one the game has taken back at 5 km, and rigs left behind on a planet. All four companions react, and the warnings count as important, so they're said even on Rare.
* **Companions that follow you into the rings.** Every companion now follows a mining trip in a ring, from the journal: the ring's hotspots once you've mapped it (and again as you drop in), poor rocks and ones already mined out, tonnage milestones (10, 25, 50, 100 tonnes and on), a half-full hold, limpets running low, and a summary of the haul as you leave ("34 tonnes refined: 25 Platinum and 9 Painite."). The hotspots and the haul wait their turn if something else was just said, rather than being lost.
* **Years said as years.** Every companion's voice now says "1998" as "nineteen ninety-eight", "2005" as "two thousand five" and Elite's "3312" as "thirty-three twelve", instead of "one thousand nine hundred and ninety-eight". Counts stay counts ("1500 tonnes", "1200 light years"), and star catalogue names keep their numbers (LHS 3447).
* **"Hello" for the whole crew.** The voice greeting is now just "hello" in Settings › Voice (it was "hello watcher"), and works with any companion's name: "hi Axiom", "hey Meridian", "hello Aster", "hello the Watcher". Greeting a crewmate who isn't on watch gets their own reply, off watch. Your saved changes to the command are kept. Axiom and Meridian now have as many greetings (50) and "what can you do" rundowns (10) as the Watcher and Aster, and their new rundowns cover route talk, survey reports, mining and the Rhino's rigs, and asking a crewmate.
* **The ship's everyday life.** Every companion now notices the moments it used to let pass: docking granted (with your pad) or denied (and why), refuelling at a station, switching ships, repairs from limpets or the AFMU (naming the module), being scanned, dropping into a signal source (named), heat and neutron-cone damage, a carrier jump scheduled or cancelled, a new Powerplay rank, synthesis (named readably: "a premium FSD injection", "limpets") and taking a screenshot. Tiny fuel top-ups after scooping, and the rank 0 Elite sends when you pledge, stay quiet.
* **Docking you can hear.** Docking was a quiet moment by design, so on Occasional and Rare your companion said nothing when you were cleared to land or touched down. Now docking is always acknowledged, even on Rare, and "docking granted" (with your pad) and undocking are said on Occasional and Chatty.
* **Your squadron, beyond its name.** Companions used to say your squadron's name and nothing more. Now they know your rank in it, write names readably ("East Orion Company", not capitals), and react to applying, invitations, joining, leaving, being kicked, promotions and demotions, creating or disbanding a squadron, trophies won for it and bookmarks shared with it. TALK answers "what squadron am I in?" and "what's my squadron rank?".
* **Distances that make sense.** The new companions said distances without units ("We are 22,000 from Sol") and always measured "from Sol", even when your home is somewhere else. Every distance now says light years, and distance from home names your actual home.
* **Chatty means chatty.** On Chatty, companions speak up more often on a run of jumps and a kind of remark can come back after three minutes instead of five. Their jump-by-jump talk (arrivals, scoops, long jumps, route milestones) starts a new round once every line has been used, never the same line twice in a row, instead of going quiet for the rest of a long trip. Everything else still never repeats, and Rare and Occasional are unchanged.
