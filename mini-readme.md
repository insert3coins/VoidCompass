# VoidCompass // UPDATE LOG

## v5.8.6 // Second Chances
**Release Date:** TBA *(Galactic Standard Time)*

* **Nova, the sixth companion.** N.O.V.A. (Navigation, Observation & Vessel Assistance) was a prototype assistant built to notice what a pilot needs before they ask. Her project was cancelled before its final evaluation and her core spent years in storage; a restored build came aboard your ship through a station's tech services. She's observant, curious and friendly, a little playful, and puzzled by detours, which she asks about rather than judges. Two styles under her commentary setting: Playful and Assistant.
  * **Her own words** for everything the others speak about, from arrivals, scans and biology to mining, the Rhino, routes, docking, combat, missions, carriers, Powerplay, squadrons and the quiet hours, with a particular eye for remarkable worlds, the Codex and life on the surface. TALK greetings, rundowns and every command, her name arc (*The User Profile*), postcard notes, keepsakes, overnight notes and the Captain's Log.
  * **Her story, *The Unfinished Build***: eight chapters and 48 entries from her development logs, opened by jumps, bodies scanned, exploration data sold, organisms analysed, dockings and long jumps (each with a time alternative), six echoes, and one request: take her to the heart of the galaxy.
  * **Her face**: an illustrated android head projected inside the instrument housing, not a 3D model. Her own loops for idle, listening, working, charging and alert; a mouth that follows the level of her own voice, with a blink over her eyes as she talks; a warm smile, delight, curiosity, thought or reassurance with the mood of what she's saying; surprise as an alert begins; faint scan lines and the odd flicker. Her own pearl bezel with a lit ring at each side and a star at the top, and her own palette, **Nova ice**. Every Studio size, focus, animation and frame-rate setting, on the BRIDGE, the Companion overlay and the boot screen.
  * **Her voice**: Cori, a British-English Piper voice, downloaded and checked like the others, with its own receipt.
  * **The crew**: every crewmate has a new Coming Aboard passage about her (added at the end, so what you've heard keeps its place), and she has eight of her own. Handovers both ways, off-watch remarks, debriefs, postscripts, a line in every Night Watch scene, crew banter with each of the five, stream chat welcomes and a place on the watch rota (no default duty: give her one on the companion page).
  * **Crew Orders**: two kinds of her own, three new Codex entries out past the bubble and a picture of a remarkable world in the system you're in, her Order File (*Page Eleven*), standing and a Right Hand ribbon, and a line in each campaign's look-back.
  * **Achievements**: Activation, Page Eleven, Every View Kept and The View from the Middle. Full Crew now needs all six companions, and The Watch Rota all 48 crew passages.
