# VoidCompass // UPDATE LOG

## v5.7.4 // All Hands on Deck
**Release Date:** 5 NOV 3312 *(Galactic Standard Time)*

* **Four companions.** Choose The Watcher, Aster, Axiom or Meridian at the top of the companion page. Each has its own character, story, voice, display and memory for every commander, and they share the same ship commands, aliases, sequences, microphone and uplink. Switching stops queued speech, ignores late replies meant for the previous companion, and leaves each one's memory, story clock and preferences untouched. See [Aster](docs/ASTER_COMPANION.md), [Axiom](docs/AXIOM_COMPANION.md), [Meridian](docs/MERIDIAN_COMPANION.md) and [setup](docs/CREW_COMPANIONS.md).
* **Aster, officer on watch.** A precise, dryly witty ship's officer with Dry wit or Restrained commentary. Its story, *Relieved of the Watch*, has 8 chapters and 48 passages: a handover book with one entry from each captain it served, and the last page is yours.
* **Axiom, survey intelligence.** Precise, literal and curious, with Curious or Precise commentary. In *The Uncounted Star* (8 chapters, 48 passages), the cataloguing mind of a twelve-station survey array finally explains the one extra light its recounts always found.
* **Meridian, veteran navigator.** Measured and steady, with Reflective or Plain commentary. In *The Last Sounding* (8 chapters, 48 passages), the navigator of an old charting fleet tells why the last line of its log was left blank.
* **The crew.** The four companions share one backstory. The Watcher was aboard first; Aster, Axiom and Meridian came aboard later as second-hand cores. They met on the ship's data bus while you were logged off, and Aster's watch rota is why one of them is on watch at a time. Each tells its own six-passage *Coming Aboard* account in quiet moments, including one passage about each crewmate once you've flown with that crewmate too. Switching companions now hands over the watch by name ("I have the watch. Thank you, Mister Aster."). Companions sometimes mention their crewmates, and the STORY tab collects all four accounts. Three achievements: *Introductions*, *Full Crew* and *The Watch Rota*.
* **The crew off watch.** Ask another companion something by name in TALK ("Meridian, how far to Colonia?") and it answers in its own words and voice without taking the watch. At a big moment (an Earth-like world, a first footfall, a record jump or distance, a close call), a crewmate you've flown with may add one line after the companion on watch: at most once a session. A companion's first watch now starts at the Watcher's Thoughts setting.
* **Stories that follow your flying.** Each chapter opens with something real from your journal: deliveries, first discoveries, dockings, long jumps, close calls, mining, returns. Every chapter also opens with enough time together, so no play style is locked out. Passages come in quiet moments, never in danger, and later chapters wait a few real days. Echoes let a real event bring back a chapter you've heard. STORY shows how each sealed chapter opens without revealing it, and the whole book can be read and saved once it's told.
* **Companions that care about their own work.** Axiom speaks up more about scans, maps, unexplored systems and Codex entries. Meridian speaks up more about arrivals, long jumps, distance from Sol and quiet cruising. Aster mentions ordinary arrivals and fuel scoops now and then. Each has many more lines for these topics, so a long night of exploring stays varied, and on Occasional they no longer go quiet through a run of jumps.
* **Learning your name.** Aster (*The Crew Register*), Axiom (*The Observer Field*) and Meridian (*The Ship's Articles*) each ask in their own words whether to use your journal name, Commander or a nickname. Answer with the panel, your answer keys, the chat or voice. Pronunciation is saved, and your choice becomes their regular form of address.
* **Plenty to say, and room for quiet.** Each new companion has its own greetings, capability rundowns, reports for every event and command result in both commentary styles, port company, learned-habit remarks, old postcards and service memories, hatchling notes, login welcomes and end-of-watch summaries. Rotations survive restarts, idle remarks follow your chosen pace, and they go quiet rather than repeat themselves. Shutdown summaries clearly describe the session just completed.
* **Keepsakes and deeper company.** Time capsules, finished constellations, On this day and the anniversary of meeting are now spoken by whichever companion is on watch. Each keeps its own readings of you (Aster's confidence and morale, Axiom's certainty and outlook, Meridian's confidence and spirits) and writes a short note after every watch, which it may mention next time. Friend codes work between any two companions. Each asks once, late in its story, to be taken somewhere that matters to it, and an expedition can make that an objective. Twelve new achievements follow their stories. The Captain's Log closing paragraph is written and signed by the companion that was on watch that session.
* **Postcards in their own hand.** Automatic postcard notes are written and signed by the active companion. Saved pictures, the editor and caption files keep the right author after a switch.
* **Companion overlay displays.** Transparent Three.js instruments: Aster's luminous halo around a hexagonal command core, Axiom's violet/cyan particle torus around a lattice crystal, and Meridian's gold/blue wireframe portrait with lips that move with real speech. All sit in the Watcher's bezel for contrast over bright scenery, react to listening, working, speech and alerts, and match the Watcher's size.
* **A Watcher that never sits still.** With idle animation on, the Watcher's eye now drifts slowly and looks around on its own every so often (a weary Watcher mostly looks down and away), its glances reach a little further, and its galaxy turns a little faster. It still dozes when nothing happens, but it no longer looks frozen beside the other companions.
* **One Studio inspector.** Size, colour, focus movement, liveliness, idle animation and frame rate work the same for every companion and apply immediately. Each companion has its own signature palette (Aster cyan, Axiom violet, Meridian gold, the Watcher's HAL red), and size carries across switches.
* **Captions in step with speech.** The floating overlay and the deck's BRIDGE type each line with the same timing, following the voice as it plays. Queued replies wait their turn, and the panel's controls stay still while text appears.
* **A cleaner companion page.** A compact A–Z switcher sits above five tabs. BRIDGE keeps your companion's display beside the conversation, with its story, your bond and recent thoughts at the side. Then STORY, YOU, LOG and SETUP (thoughts, voice and reset together). The chat names the companion that replied, with example commands and the input always at hand.
* **Voices.** Aster, Axiom and Meridian each download their own checksum-verified Piper voice and share one engine. Installing or removing one never breaks another, and each has its own test line on the VOICE page.
* **Your companion on the boot screen.** Startup presents the companion you chose with its own display, instead of always the Watcher.
* **FOLLOW ME on the galaxy map.** A new Atlas button keeps your position centred as you jump, at your current zoom. Hover over it for a description; drag the map to stop following.
* **Companions in the Local API.** New `/api/v1/companion` and `/api/v1/companions` routes read, select and talk to any companion, refuse a request meant for a companion that is no longer active, and name the companion in every reply and event. The `/watcher` routes keep working for the active companion. See [api.md](api.md#companions).
* **Fixes.** Login welcomes on every new game session, including quick relogs, without doubling up. Port conversation starts after a docked startup. Hatchling remarks are paced and never replayed. An invalid saved animation setting now falls back to its default.
