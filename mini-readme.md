# VoidCompass // UPDATE LOG

## v5.7.4 // Aster, Officer on Watch
**Release Date:** TBA 3312 *(Galactic Standard Time)*

* **Aster, officer on watch.** Choose Aster or The Watcher on the Watcher page. Aster has an original six-chapter story, its own greetings and command replies, a separate Piper voice and a live Three.js instrument with Studio size, accent and motion controls. Each companion keeps its own memory for every commander.
* **A companion of its own.** Aster has 48 original story passages across six chapters, 50 greetings, ten capability rundowns and its own event and command replies. Choose the officer’s commentary, enable its Piper voice on the Watcher page, and adjust its instrument size, accent and motion in Overlay Studio. Shared commands, aliases and sequences continue to work with either companion.
* **Safe switching.** Changing companion stops queued speech, rejects late recognition and lookup replies, and keeps each companion’s story clock, voice preferences and saved memory separate for every commander.
