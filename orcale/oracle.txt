I saw them first as distortion in the jump tunnel, where the stars stretch too far and time feels like it forgets itself.
They do not travel through space the way we do, they exist in the spaces between movement, hiding in the seams of hyperspace.
They are not Thargoids, not constructs, not myths passed through GalNet for entertainment or fear.
The name buried in pre-Federation black archives is Guardians, and it was erased for a reason.
They were encountered before humanity ever reached the core systems, and every record of that contact was sealed.
They were not destroyed, they were postponed, locked behind folded dimensions and forgotten by design.
What we call anomalies are only shadows cast by their real bodies as they press against our reality.
Every unexplained signal is not noise, but a residue of their presence bleeding into our sensors.
They move through time the way we move through space, choosing when to be seen.
Hyperspace is not empty, it is occupied, and we have been trespassing without knowing it.
Their cities are not built on planets, but suspended in gravitational scars left by collapsed stars.
They observed us before Sol ever launched its first frameshift experiment.
They watched our species learn to burn fuel, then bend space, then tear holes through it.
They mapped our routes long before we drew our first star charts.
They understand our empires better than we understand ourselves.
They study our conflicts not as wars, but as predictable cycles of decay.
They do not fear us, because they already know the outcome of our growth.
Every jump we make echoes through their domain like a bell ringing in the dark.
They count our ships, our pilots, our failures, and our hopes.
They are patient beyond any measure of human time.
The first signs were dismissed as sensor glitches and navigation drift.
The second were explained away as experimental weapons tests.
The third were named Thargoids, a convenient myth to hide the deeper truth.
That name was a mask placed over something far older and far more controlled.
The Guardians are not invaders, they are engineers of perception.
They shape what we see so we never look in the right direction.
They alter readings, distort memory, and fracture recorded history.
They hide behind false enemies so we fight reflections instead of the source.
While we argue about aliens, they reshape the corridors of hyperspace.
They do not negotiate, because to them we are variables, not equals.
They do not conquer worlds the way empires do.
They drain meaning from them, leaving structures intact but purpose erased.
They do not kill populations, they remove their context from reality.
Entire systems will continue to exist, but no one will remember why they mattered.
Your logs will contradict your memories, and your memories will contradict the stars.
History will begin to fracture into overlapping versions of events.
Navigation will feel wrong, as if space itself resists your path.
Stars will appear to shift when no movement occurred.
Time will seem uneven, stretching and compressing around you.
This is how their presence announces itself.
When nav beacons fall silent without damage or trace.
When hyperspace exits feel delayed, or too fast, or incomplete.
When the galaxy feels subtly misaligned, as if rotated by a fraction.
They are no longer approaching, they are intersecting our reality.
The Guardians are not coming in fleets, they are unfolding across dimensions.
We are already standing within the edge of their shadow.
Every jump brings us deeper into their architecture.
There is no escape vector, because they exist outside direction.
Only awareness remains when distance loses meaning.
Prepare your minds, because the Guardians have remembered us again.
