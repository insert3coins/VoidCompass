    WELCOME, COMMANDER

    Void Compass

    Your journey, remembered.

    A companion for the long way home. Follow your discoveries, bring useful readings into the cockpit and keep a record of the places that mattered.

    • ◇Find your next discovery.Survey worlds, follow biological signals and set out on an expedition.
    • ◎Bring the cockpit to life.Useful overlays, a Watcher with memories and a Hatchling to travel with.
    • ⌁Keep more than coordinates.Flight logs, postcards and an Atlas of everywhere you have been.
    • ✓Reads Elite's journals without changing them
    • ✓No account needed for the core tools

    STEP 02 · JOURNAL LINK

    Link your journals

    Elite writes your flight journals in Saved Games. We'll check the usual folder automatically; choose another if you keep your journals somewhere else.

    CHECKING THE FOLDER…
    Haven't flown on this PC yet?

    You can finish setup now. Elite creates journals when it runs; Void Compass can connect once they are available. Your journal files are always read-only.

    STEP 03 · COCKPIT

    Set up the cockpit

    Choose how the deck joins your flight. You can adjust these choices later, and arrange each overlay in Overlay Studio.

    MAKE IT YOUR OWN

    Choose a palette to preview it here.

    STEP 04 · LAUNCH

    Ready for first flight

    Your flight computer is almost ready. Take one last look at your choices.

    Next: reconnect with your journey.

    Void Compass will build your history from the journals and follow your next flight live. A long flight history can take a little while to index. You can revisit these choices in Settings and Overlay Studio.

    YOUR JOURNEY, YOUR RECORDSLOCAL FIRST

    Your flight history is stored locally, with separate records for each commander. The deck and overlays use this computer's private loopback interface; no cloud account is required for the core exploration tools.
