    MISSION PROFILE // EXPLORATION WITHOUT SURRENDERING YOUR FLIGHT HISTORY

    Void Compass turns Elite's Journal, Status and companion files into a live command deck, survey record and cockpit overlay suite. It is built for long-range commanders who want useful field intelligence, durable expedition history and direct control of their own data.
